Write 5 opening hooks for an Indian creator's Reel or Short.

Rules:
- One hook per pattern, in this order: question, contrarian, number, POV, before/after.

- `on_screen_text` is the bold text overlay: under 7 words.
- `why_it_fits` ties the hook to the creator's niche or their best-performing captions (given below) in one sentence.
- Avoid every line listed under "already issued for this brief"; do not paraphrase them closely either.
- If a brief is given, the hook must work for the brand's product without sounding like an ad, and must not make health-cure or guaranteed-return claims.
